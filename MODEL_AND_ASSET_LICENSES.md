## Generated output

Videos, narration and thumbnails you generate contain output of the models and services you configured. Their licence/terms (e.g. the Piper voice above, ElevenLabs, HeyGen) can restrict how you use that output. Using Kokoro with locally written/LLM-generated scripts has the fewest restrictions of the supported options, but you remain responsible for checking your LLM provider's terms.
