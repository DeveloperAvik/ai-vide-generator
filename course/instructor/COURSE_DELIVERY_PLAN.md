# Course Delivery Plan

## Cohort-based delivery (12-week default)

One module per week, synchronous Q&A session after each module's lessons are watched async, module assessment due before the next week's session. Capstone (Module 12) gets 2 weeks.

| Week | Module | Live session focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | Kill chain/ATT&CK walkthrough using a fictional incident |
| 2 | 2 | Live lab-build troubleshooting (VM networking is the #1 blocker) |
| 3 | 3 | PE/ELF internals Q&A, code-along review |
| 4 | 4 | Obfuscation technique review |
| 5 | 5 | Group reverse-engineering walkthrough |
| 6 | 6 | C2/traffic-analysis walkthrough |
| 7 | 7 | Debugger clinic (GDB/x64dbg) |
| 8 | 8 | ROP-chain building session |
| 9 | 9 | Persistence detection walkthrough |
| 10 | 10 | Case-study discussion, ATT&CK mapping exercise |
| 11 | 11 | Detection-engineering lab session |
| 12–13 | 12 | Capstone office hours + final presentations |

## Self-paced delivery

Learner follows `LEARNING_PATH.md` pacing table; module assessments are self-graded against the published answer keys/rubrics; capstone is submitted for instructor review on completion.

## Instructor prerequisites

An instructor delivering Modules 7–9 live should be comfortable troubleshooting debugger/VM issues in real time; keep `requirements/ENVIRONMENT.md` and each lab's troubleshooting section open during live sessions.
